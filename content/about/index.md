## Systems foundation

Linux is the center of my systems background. I work across compute, networking, storage, and virtualized environments, with an emphasis on understanding failure modes and making operational procedures explicit.

## Automation and infrastructure as code

I use **Bash**, **Python**, and **Golang** to remove repetitive work and build operational tooling. **Ansible** and **Terraform** turn system configuration and infrastructure provisioning into reviewable, repeatable processes.

## Containers, data platforms, and delivery

My platform work spans containers, **Docker**, **containerd**, **Kubernetes**, and **Helm**, together with the CI/CD systems that move changes safely toward production. I also operate stateful platforms including **PostgreSQL**, **MariaDB**, **Redis**, **Kafka**, **MinIO**, **GlusterFS**, and **ZFS**, with attention to replication, failover, performance, and recovery.

## Operating with feedback

Reliable systems need useful signals. I use **Prometheus**, **Grafana**, **ELK**, **Sentry**, and **Zabbix** to make behavior visible through metrics, logs, dashboards, and alerts—shortening diagnosis and improving platforms using what production teaches us.

## How I approach systems

- Automate repetitive work without hiding important decisions
- Prefer observable systems over operational guesswork
- Make dangerous procedures explicit, validated, and reversible
- Treat networking, storage, and operating-system behavior as part of the platform
- Keep infrastructure understandable as it grows
- Share tools and lessons that can save someone else time

One example is my interactive Bash utility for [safer Kubernetes node upgrades](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/interactive-bash-script-safe-custom-kubernetes-node-mehdi-frzzf). It detects node roles, guides component selection, validates requested versions, and handles the differing needs of worker and control-plane nodes.
